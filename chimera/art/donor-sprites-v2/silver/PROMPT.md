# silver（bulwark）戰場 sprite v2

## 提示詞

Use the three reference images: the first two are the game's existing sprites and define the STYLE and CAMERA only (overhead three-quarter front/down-facing camera, chunky SNES pixel-art proportions, strong dark outline, high-contrast shading). The third image is the CHARACTER to draw: a young woman with long brown braids, a dark knit beanie, an olive parka. She is a heavy armored trooper: chunky layered armor plates and huge armored mechanical gauntlet arms worn over her outfit, a light machine gun, the broadest heaviest silhouette. Create a TWO-SPRITE sheet, two figures side by side in two equal cells with a wide empty gap between them so they never overlap. LEFT: this woman STANDING, full body. RIGHT: the SAME woman DEAD, collapsed on the floor, clearly horizontal, knocked down from the left, weapon lying beside her; a genuinely different fallen pose, no gore, no blood. CRUCIAL: draw each figure as a real 32 by 32 pixel sprite enlarged exactly 24 times: every sprite pixel is one clean solid square block, about 24 by 24 screen pixels, all blocks aligned to one grid, no in-between colors, no smaller detail than one block. Use about 12 flat colors with 3 shades per material (dark, mid, light), a 1-block near-black outline around the whole silhouette, distinct value separation between hair, skin, main outfit and boots so each reads at 32 pixels. No text, no grid lines drawn, no environment, no floor, no shadow, no extra characters. The whole background is one flat solid pure green color (#00FF00), nothing else on it; do not use that green on the characters.
